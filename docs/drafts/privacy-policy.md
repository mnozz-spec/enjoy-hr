## 2. The short version

- You can read the site without an account. There are no user accounts for readers and no comments.
- We do not sell personal data for money.
- The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and Google AdSense to show ads. Both use cookies or similar identifiers, and both depend on the choice you make in the consent message where one is shown to you.
- If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is stored with Mailchimp.
- If you use the contact form, your message is emailed to us and is not saved on the website.
- Some links are affiliate links. Clicking one takes you through a tracking link operated by CJ Affiliate.

- Legal basis (GDPR): legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): operating and securing the site.

### 3.2 Analytics (Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager)

We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used: which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from, and what kind of device they use. Google Tag Manager is the tool that loads the analytics script. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers and collects usage data together with online identifiers and approximate location derived from your IP address.

- Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) where consent is required by law, for example in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland.
- Retention: [TODO Marko: check the data retention setting in the Google Analytics property (for example 2 or 14 months) and state it here.]
- Provider: Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. See [how Google uses data from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 3.3 Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site is funded partly by ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. Depending on your choice and your location, ads may be personalised (based on your earlier visits to this and other sites) or non-personalised (based on context, such as the page you are reading).

Where the law requires it, a consent message provided by Google (Funding Choices) asks for your choice before personalised advertising is used. You can change that choice later; see section 6.

- Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for advertising cookies and personalised ads where consent is required; otherwise legitimate interests in funding a free site.
- Retention: controlled by Google and its partners. See [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### 3.4 Newsletter (Mailchimp)

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address and store it with Mailchimp (Intuit Inc., USA), which sends the emails on our behalf. Mailchimp also records technical data connected to the subscription, such as the time of sign-up, and whether emails are opened or links are clicked.

- Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email.
- Retention: until you unsubscribe. [TODO Marko: decide and state how long unsubscribed addresses are kept on the suppression list, or say that they are deleted on request.]

### 3.5 Contact form

When you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message by email. The form does not save the message in the website's database. To limit spam, the site keeps a short-lived counter tied to a one-way hash of your IP address for about one hour; the IP address itself is not stored by the form.

- Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in answering your enquiry, or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) where that applies.
- Retention: messages stay in our mailbox for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. [TODO Marko: state a concrete period, for example 12 months, and name the email provider that hosts the mailbox.]

### 3.6 Affiliate links (CJ Affiliate, Booking.com)

Some links to Booking.com are affiliate links that go through CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction LLC). We do not set affiliate cookies when you merely read a page. When you click an affiliate link, CJ and the merchant may set cookies or use similar tracking on their own domains so that a booking can be attributed to this site. We receive reports about clicks and commissions, not your name, contact details or payment data.

- Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in funding the site; the tracking itself happens after your click, on the third party's site, under their own privacy policies.
- More detail: our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/), the [CJ privacy policy](https://www.cj.com/legal/privacy) and the [Booking.com privacy statement](https://www.booking.com/content/privacy.html).

### 3.7 Fonts (Google Fonts)

The site loads its fonts from Google's servers. To deliver them, your browser sends Google your IP address and basic browser information. No cookies are set for this.

- Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in displaying the site consistently.

### 3.8 WordPress cookies

The site runs on WordPress. WordPress sets login and session cookies only for the site's administrators when they sign in. Ordinary readers do not get these cookies.

## 7. Your rights (EU, EEA and UK readers)

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to:

- ask for access to the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have data erased;
- have processing restricted;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- receive data you gave us in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We reply within one month. In practice, we hold very little data that identifies you: your email address if you subscribed, and your message if you wrote to us. Data held by Google or CJ under their own identifiers is best managed with the tools in section 6.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Croatia this is the Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), [azop.hr](https://azop.hr/). In the UK it is the Information Commissioner's Office, [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/). You may also contact the authority in the country where you live.

# About Enjoy Croatia

Enjoy Croatia is an independent, English-language guide for people visiting Croatia. It is written from Croatia, for readers who are planning a trip from abroad and need to know how things work here.

## What we cover

We write about the practical side of a trip: ferries and catamarans, driving and tolls, airports and transfers, safety, and the local rules that visitors often learn about too late. The aim of each guide is simple. You should be able to act on it without opening five other tabs.

## How we check facts

Timetables, prices and regulations in Croatia change often, sometimes from one season to the next. For that reason our guides carry a "checked" date. ## How the site is funded

Enjoy Croatia is free to read. It is paid for by display advertising and by affiliate links. If you book through an affiliate link, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Neither ads nor commissions affect what we recommend. A guide says the same thing whether or not a link in it earns us anything. The details are in our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
